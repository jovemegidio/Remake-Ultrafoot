## Player Data

Player names, ages, nationalities, and performance ratings included in this simulation are
drawn from community-curated databases for fan-made purposes. This data is **not official**
and does not originate from EA Sports, Konami/EFOOTBALL, or any official football body.

All player names are the intellectual property of the respective individuals and/or **FIFPro**
(Fédération Internationale des Associations de Footballeurs Professionnels), which represents
professional footballers globally.

Data source: community database `github.com/jovemegidio/Ultrafoot` (dataset version 2026-27).
